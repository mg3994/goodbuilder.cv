Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Last Updated — Good Builder CV (goodbuilder.cv) by Antinna

1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Good Builder CV (operated by Antinna, founded by Manish). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your data when you visit and use our web application located at goodbuilder.cv.

2. Client-Side Storage & Firebase Cloud Sync

Good Builder CV is designed with a privacy-first architecture:

  • Local Browser Storage: Unauthenticated users process and store all resume details locally in their web browser using localStorage. No data is transmitted to external servers without explicit user request.
  • Firebase Auth & Cloud Sync: If you choose to sign in with Google Auth to use our Cloud Sync & Share feature, your authentication identity is processed securely via Firebase Authentication. Publishing your resume creates a dedicated online post on our Blogger blog, generating an authentic scannable QR Code pointing to your published portfolio post URL.
  • User Control & Limit: Users can publish up to 5 free resumes attached to their account UID. You can update or delete your published blog post resumes at any time.

3. Log Files & Web Analytics

Good Builder CV uses standard log files and non-personally identifiable analytics to monitor website performance and traffic patterns. Information collected includes IP addresses, browser type, referring pages, date/time stamps, and click counts to analyze trends and administer the site.

4. Advertising Networks (Monetag & Google AdSense)

Good Builder CV works with third-party advertising networks, including Monetag and Google AdSense, to serve non-intrusive advertisements across our platform:

  • Monetag Advertising Network: We use Monetag as an official advertising monetization partner. Monetag and its technology partners may collect non-personally identifiable information, cookies, device identifiers, and web beacons to serve ads, optimize ad delivery, and prevent fraud. Use of Monetag ad services on Good Builder CV is subject to the Monetag Privacy Policy and Monetag Terms of Service.
  • Google AdSense & DART Cookies: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DART cookie) to serve ads based on users visits to our website. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or www.aboutads.info.

5. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Under regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA, you possess rights including the right to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal data. You can clear your local data at any time using the built-in Clear tool.
