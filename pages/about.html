About Good Builder CV & Antinna

Empowering Job Seekers Worldwide with Free, Privacy-First Resume Engineering

Who We Are

Good Builder CV (goodbuilder.cv) is an advanced, free web application developed by Antinna and created by software engineer and tech entrepreneur Manish. Our core mission is to make professional resume creation, ATS optimization, and cover letter writing accessible to job seekers globally—without paywalls, hidden subscriptions, or user tracking.

We believe your career information belongs solely to you. Unlike traditional resume builders that store your personal details on remote servers, Good Builder CV operates with a 100% privacy-first client-side architecture. Your data is stored locally in your browser and converted into clean PDF, Word, and HTML documents instantly.

Key Innovations & Features

🎨 13+ Built-in Templates

Modern, Executive, Minimalist, Europass, Sleek Dark, Timeline, and Infographic designs tailored for all industries.

🛠️ Custom Template Studio

Design, save, import, and export custom HTML/CSS template structures with placeholder tokens.

📱 Firebase Cloud Sync & QR Sharing

Sign in with Google to publish your CV online as a blog post (/YYYY/MM/title.html) and generate a scannable portfolio QR code.

📊 ATS Match & Proofreader

Real-time ATS score calculator, job description keyword density analyzer, and bullet point quality proofreader.

📄 Multi-Format Exports

Download vector-sharp PDFs via html2pdf.js, Microsoft Word .doc files, standalone HTML, and JSON backups.
