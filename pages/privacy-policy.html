Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Last Updated — Good Builder CV (goodbuilder.cv) by Antinna

1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Good Builder CV (operated by Antinna, founded by Manish). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your data when you visit and use our web application located at goodbuilder.cv.

By accessing or using Good Builder CV, you signify that you have read, understood, and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Client-Side Resume Data & LocalStorage

Good Builder CV is designed with a privacy-first, client-side architecture:

  • Local Browser Storage: All resume data, personal details, work experience, education, skills, photos, and custom template designs you enter into the builder are processed entirely in your web browser and saved locally using localStorage.
  • No Server Database Uploads: We do not upload, transmit, or store your private CV or resume details on any remote database servers. Your personal career information remains on your own device under your sole control.

3. Log Files & Web Analytics

Like most standard web platforms, Good Builder CV uses standard log files and non-personally identifiable analytics to monitor website performance and traffic patterns. Information collected includes Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), referring/exit pages, date/time stamps, and click counts. This data is not linked to any personally identifiable information and is used solely to analyze trends, administer the site, and improve user experience.

4. Advertising Networks (Monetag & Google AdSense)

Good Builder CV works with third-party advertising networks, including Monetag and Google AdSense, to serve non-intrusive advertisements across our platform:

  • Monetag Advertising Network: We use Monetag as an official advertising monetization partner. Monetag and its technology partners may collect non-personally identifiable information, cookies, device identifiers, and web beacons to serve ads, optimize ad delivery, and prevent fraud. Use of Monetag ad services on Good Builder CV is subject to the Monetag Privacy Policy and Monetag Terms of Service.
  • Google AdSense & DART Cookies: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DART cookie) to serve ads based on users visits to our website. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or www.aboutads.info.

We use Google AdSense to serve non-intrusive advertisements when you visit our website. Google, as a third-party advertising vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on Good Builder CV:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to our site or other sites on the Internet.
  • Google uses the DART cookie to serve interest-based ads. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. Third-Party CDN Resources

To deliver high performance and rich features, Good Builder CV loads reliable third-party open-source libraries from reputable Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) including Google Fonts, Font Awesome, html2pdf.js, and qrcodejs. These external assets do not gather or process your resume information.

6. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Under regulations such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you possess rights including the right to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal data. Because Good Builder CV stores your resume data locally in your browser's storage, you can exercise your right to erasure at any time simply by clicking the "Clear" button inside the application toolbar or clearing your web browser's cache and local storage.

7. Children's Information

Protecting children's privacy on the internet is extremely important to us. Good Builder CV does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will promptly take appropriate action.
